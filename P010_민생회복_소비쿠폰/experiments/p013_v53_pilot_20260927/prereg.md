# P013 v53 paired pilot — frozen before the first policy arm

- Purpose: diagnose whether the generic v53 citizen prompt yields an auditable, policy-sensitive paired ON/OFF signal. This is **not** an empirical magnitude validation or an optimal-prompt selection.
- Policy: frozen `data/experiments/P013_v53_policy_20260926.json` only. No P015 replay. Both arms use identical code, EXAONE-4.5-33B-AWQ, `covid_2021` background, v53 Stage1/Stage2, 80 decile-stratified citizens, fixed personal daily budget, and 39-day opening balance convention. No Stage2 fallback is allowed.
- Calendar: 2020-05-09 to 2020-05-13 inclusive; policy effective 2020-05-11; primary effect window 2020-05-11 to 2020-05-13. This 3-day exposure cannot represent the published grant evaluation period.
- Arm order: OFF first, export complete daily ledger and hash; reset graph, seed same Day0, load policy, preflight DB wiring; ON second, export complete ledger and hash. A verified full Neo4j restore dump on server/G:/C: precedes reset.
- Budget-map missing-data rule: a missing weekday or weekend anchor uses the observed counterpart; if both are absent, use a fixed 1,500,000/39 won per day. Record the count of this synthetic fallback. The rule is identical in both arms and does not use policy outcomes.
- Primary technical gates: 80 unique same agents × 5 days per arm; no request failures; Stage2 quality gate; no fallback; ON policy exposure and grant receipt, none in OFF; equal execution/prompt/income-map fingerprints; wallet and own-spend reconciled. A failed gate invalidates effect estimates.
- Exploratory paired outcomes: total and eligible-offline recorded spending ON−OFF, relative change, incremental recorded spending per grant won, recipient/wallet reconciliation, citizen bootstrap 95% interval. All are simulation internal and proxy measures. No empirical reference is used in the run or prompt.
- Report: show every registered policy indicator, including not-run ones, and state separately whether increasing N alone could help, whether a longer policy exposure is necessary, and whether an external outcome/definition or measurement change is required. Do not compute a numeric empirical error across unmatched estimands. The historical P012 diagnostic and technical screens do not count as ON/OFF evidence.
